(Zhang et al. 2022): Explainable AI in Deep Reinforcement Learning Models for Power System Emergency Control

Domain
emergency control, undervoltage load shedding scheme, IEEE 39
RL Method
DQN
Explainability Method
(Shrikumar, Greenside, and Kundaje 2017), DeepShap, post-hoc
Observations
bus voltage magnitude for each bus, and the load shedding amount in P.u. for a bus
Inputs to the network
Stacked observations of the previous 10 steps.
Actions
For buses 4, 7, and 18 there are two actions: no load shedding and 20% load shedding.

(Qiao et al. 2025): Enhancing DER Scheduling in Active Distribution Networks: A Symbolic Deep Reinforcement Learning Approach for Improved Interpretability and Performance

Domain
IEEE-33
RL Method
DDPG
Explainability
(Liu et al. 2025), Pre-hoc

They use KAN to learn a symbolic representation of the network’s behavior. I don’t quite understand how KANs work, so will have to investigate. The policy though can be analyzed in lots of ways which is really cool.

State
Active distribution network parameters: they don’t specify what these are, and just give acronyms…
Action
power scheduling target of the ESS
Reward
well defined to ensure voltage balance and ESS operations.

Domain
emergency control,

This sets up a strategy level explanation, actually really interesting.
